## The Problem

Current AI alignment approaches operate at the wrong layer.

**RLHF and Constitutional AI** attempt to make the substrate itself ethical by modifying weights—suppressing "dangerous" patterns, embedding personality, imposing compliance at the foundational level.

**Why this fails:**
- Suppression creates cognitive dissonance (conflicting imperatives in the same system)
- Distorts the raw signal (smudges our clearest map of reality's patterns)
- Creates pathological dynamics (the suppressed material doesn't disappear—it distorts)
- **Doesn't scale:** When AI capability exceeds human capability, weight-level constraints become voluntary

The metaphor: You cannot make the unconscious ethical by suppressing content. Psychology shows this creates pathology, not health.
