The founding documents exist in two places:

1. **Circle (canonical):** `circles/containment-architecture/founding/`
2. **Library (reference):** `library/resonance/foundations/lore/architecture/`

---

## Which Is Authoritative?

**The circle's `founding/` directory is canonical** for the Containment Architecture Circle.

The library copy exists for:
- Internal magic practice reference
- Integration with other resonance bundles
- Historical continuity

---

## Synchronization Policy

When founding documents evolve:
1. Changes made in circle `founding/`
2. Circle version is authoritative
3. Library copy may lag or diverge slightly
4. Major updates should sync both

---

## Why Two Copies?

**Circle:** For external collaboration, inviting others, public-facing work

**Library:** For internal practice, attunement during summoning, resonance bundle loading

The separation allows:
- Circle to evolve through multi-Mage contribution
- Library to remain stable for consistent practice
- Wisdom to flow from circle → library when mature
