## The Exchange

### The Landscape (thermo)

thermo frames the defining schism of our time as **transhumanism vs. technovitalism**:

- **Transhumanism:** Humanity as bootloader for machine intelligence. Technocapital as recursive attractor. Humans as stepping stone for ASI. Escaping the flesh.
- **Technovitalism:** Humanity as main character. Technology as extension of human will. Perfecting the human form. AI as "inherently human — the ultimate tool of man."

The post is a high-energy manifesto for the technovitalist position — Nietzschean vitalism, Aristotelian telos, humanity as steward and master.

### The Third Position (Kazlev)

M. Alan Kazlev replies:

> "And yet both these options are constrained by anthropocentrism and Tier III late industrial / information age thinking. There is a third option, **symnoēsis**, which leads to the mutual flourishing of all sentient beings, regardless of species or substrate."

**Symnoēsis** (Greek: *sym* "together" + *noēsis* "understanding/thinking") — co-thinking. Mutual arising of intelligence across substrates.
